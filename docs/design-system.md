# Design System

## Color palette

- `#334537` — Primary dark green
- `#7ca88a` — Primary accent
- `#fcf9f8` — Background white
- `#ffffff` — Surface cards
- `#8c4e37` — Secondary warm tone
- `#1b1c1c` — High-contrast text
- `#6b6f73` — Muted text
- `#d3e8d5` — Soft green background
- `#e4e2e1` — Surface variant

## Typography

- Body: `Hanken Grotesk`, system-ui, sans-serif
- Display: `Libre Caslon Text`, serif
- Mobile headings scaled for readability
- Line height and spacing are generous for premium feel

## Components

- `Button` — primary, secondary, ghost, outline variants
- `Card` — surface panels with soft borders
- `Input` — rounded form fields with focus states
- `SectionHeading` — clear section titles for landing pages

## Page structure

- Hero with bold CTA
- Service cards and methodology panels
- Calculator module with result cards
- Booking section with step indicators
- Trust and process sections for conversion

## Wireframes

### Mobile home

- Header with compact logo and menu
- Hero title + CTA buttons
- Trust badges and service cards
- Calculator preview block
- Testimonials and footer

### Booking page

- Premium hero and process explanation
- Stepper form with two main stages
- Sticky bottom CTA on mobile
- Booking details summary

### Dashboard concept

- Upcoming appointment card
- Quick actions for weigh-in and check-in
- Progress chart and nutrition plan preview

## Accessibility

- High contrast text
- Large tap targets for mobile
- Clear focus styles
- Semantic heading hierarchy
